Every home should feel the joy of Eid.

Baraka is a student-led team in Rabat offering Eid al-Adha sheep to Moroccan families who cannot afford one, so every household can celebrate together.

Donate a sheepEarn ajr

Why we started

For many families, the price of a sheep is out of reach. For a child, waking up on Eid with no sheep at home can feel like being left out of the celebration.

We are just getting started: a volunteer team of students in Rabat. Every dirham donated goes directly to buying sheep, and we will share how each donation was spent.

A sheep is slipping out of reach

Eid al-Adha is the biggest family celebration of the year, but a long drought and rising prices have made the sheep a heavy burden. Here is what the numbers say.

55%

of families surveyed by the Moroccan Center for Citizenship in 2024 said they struggled to afford a sheep and the tools to prepare it.

12.6%

of Moroccan households did not perform the Eid sacrifice in a 2022 national survey, up from 4.7% in 2014.

38%

fall in Morocco’s sheep and cattle herds since 2016, as drought thinned pastures and raised feed costs.

65%

of the red meat eaten in a year by the least affluent households comes from the Eid sacrifice (65.4%).

The monthly minimum wage in Morocco is 3,000 dirhams (about $300). A good domestic sheep can cost more than a month’s earnings, and many families take out loans to buy one.

Why giving a sheep matters

No child left out

On Eid morning, children share the excitement of the sheep at home. A gift means a child is not the only one without it.

Meat for the year

For the poorest households, Eid is a rare chance to eat meat. One sheep feeds a family and can be shared with neighbors.

Relief from debt

Families often borrow money to buy a sheep. Your gift lifts that pressure so they can celebrate without worry.

Earn ajr and hasanat from Allah

Giving to those in need is one of the most beloved acts in Islam. When you help a family celebrate Eid, you give sadaqah, and you hope for the reward and blessing (hasanat) that Allah promises to those who give for His sake.

Those who spend their wealth in the way of Allah are like a grain that grows seven ears, each with a hundred grains, and Allah multiplies for whom He wills.Quran, Surah Al-Baqarah 2:261
Whoever relieves a believer of a hardship in this world, Allah will relieve them of a hardship on the Day of Resurrection.Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, Sahih Muslim
Allah helps His servant as long as the servant is helping their brother.Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, Sahih Muslim

One sheep can bring a family joy, a full table, and a prayer for the one who gave it. May Allah accept from us all.

What we do

Sponsor a sheep

Donors fund a sheep for a family in need. Every contribution, small or large, brings us closer to one more sheep.

Finding families

We work with neighbors and local associations to identify families who cannot afford a sheep, and we keep their names private.

Delivered with dignity

Each sheep is brought to the family’s door before Eid, so they celebrate at home like everyone else.
